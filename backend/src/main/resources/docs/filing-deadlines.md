# GST Return Filing Deadlines and Late Fees

## GSTR-1 frequency and due dates
Under Section 37 of the CGST Act, 2017 and Rule 59 of the CGST Rules, 2017, normal registered taxpayers report outward sales in FORM GSTR-1 [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 37(1), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter9/section37_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Monthly filers (mandatory if previous-year aggregate turnover exceeds Rs. 5 crore) must file GSTR-1 by the 11th of the next month [Source: GST Portal FAQ on FORM GSTR-1A, Page 1, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/creative_faqs_on_gstr1a_fo_cr25785.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Small taxpayers with previous-year turnover up to Rs. 5 crore may opt for the Quarterly Return Monthly Payment (QRMP) scheme and file GSTR-1 quarterly by the 13th of the month after the quarter [Source: CBIC Circular No. 143/13/2020-GST dated 10-11-2020, Paras 3 & 5.1, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/Circular_Refund_143_11_2020.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. QRMP filers can optionally upload B2B invoices (up to Rs. 50 lakh per month) for Month 1 and Month 2 using the Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF) between the 1st and 13th of the next month [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 59(2)–(3), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter8/rule59_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## GSTR-3B frequency and due dates
Under Section 39(1) of the CGST Act, 2017 and Rule 61 of the CGST Rules, 2017, normal taxpayers file a summary return and pay tax in FORM GSTR-3B [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 39(1), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter9/section39_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Monthly filers must file GSTR-3B by the 20th of the next month [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 61(1)(i), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter8/rule61_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Quarterly QRMP filers must file GSTR-3B by the 22nd of the month after the quarter if their principal place of business is in Group 1 States/UTs (including Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and 4 UTs), or by the 24th for Group 2 States/UTs (including Delhi, UP, West Bengal, Bihar, Rajasthan, Punjab, Haryana, Odisha, Jharkhand, Assam, and North-East/northern States and UTs) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 61(1)(ii) Table, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter8/rule61_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. QRMP taxpayers deposit monthly tax for Month 1 and Month 2 via FORM GST PMT-06 by the 25th of the next month [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 61(3), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter8/rule61_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Composition Scheme returns (CMP-08 and GSTR-4)
Under Section 39(2) of the CGST Act, 2017 and Rule 62 of the CGST Rules, 2017, Composition Scheme taxpayers pay self-assessed tax quarterly using FORM GST CMP-08 by the 18th of the month after each quarter (18 July, 18 October, 18 January, and 18 April) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 62(1)(i), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter8/rule62_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. They file one annual return in FORM GSTR-4 [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 62(1)(ii), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter8/rule62_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. By Notification No. 12/2024-Central Tax dated 10 July 2024, the GSTR-4 due date was permanently extended from 30th April to 30th June following the financial year, starting from FY 2024-25 onwards [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Proviso to Rule 62(1), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter8/rule62_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Late fee basics and current caps
Section 47(1) of the CGST Act, 2017 prescribes a statutory late fee of Rs. 100 per day under CGST (capped at Rs. 5,000), plus an equal SGST late fee [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 47(1), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter9/section47_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Under current waiver notifications, the daily late fee for GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and GSTR-4 is reduced to Rs. 50 per day total (Rs. 25 CGST + Rs. 25 SGST) for normal returns and Rs. 20 per day total (Rs. 10 CGST + Rs. 10 SGST) for Nil returns [Source: GST Portal GSTR-3B Manual, Section 6.1(j), https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/Create_and_Submit_GSTR3B.htm, accessed 2026-11-20]. Per the 43rd GST Council rationalization (effective June 2021 / FY 2021-22 onwards), total late fees per return are capped at Rs. 500 for Nil GSTR-1/3B/4, Rs. 2,000 for GSTR-1/3B with turnover up to Rs. 1.5 crore and non-Nil GSTR-4, Rs. 5,000 for turnover above Rs. 1.5 crore up to Rs. 5 crore, and Rs. 10,000 above Rs. 5 crore [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 1722578, 43rd GST Council Meeting decisions, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1722578, accessed 2026-11-20] [Source: Notification No. 20/2021-Central Tax dated 01-06-2021, Table for GSTR-1 caps, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/central-tax/notfctn-20-central-tax-hindi-2021.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. There is no late fee on FORM GST CMP-08 or PMT-06 [Source: GST Portal CMP-08 FAQs, Q.9, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/FAQs_CMP02.htm?rhsearch=3B&rhhlterm=3b, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Composition Scheme turnover limit
Under Section 10(1) of the CGST Act, 2017 and Notification No. 14/2019-Central Tax dated 7 March 2019, the annual aggregate turnover limit for the Composition Scheme for goods suppliers and restaurants is Rs. 1.5 crore in the preceding financial year in general States and UTs [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10(1), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20] [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 1567975, Notification No. 14/2019-CT summary, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1567975, accessed 2026-11-20]. The limit is Rs. 75 lakh in 8 Special Category States: Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, and Uttarakhand [Source: GST Portal Welcome Kit for New Taxpayers, Section 2.3, p. 14, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/welcome_kit_for_new_taxpyers.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. For service providers and mixed suppliers under Section 10(2A), the turnover limit is Rs. 50 lakh across all States and UTs [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10(2A), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Recent return filing updates (2024–2026)
From July 2024 (Notification No. 12/2024-CT), taxpayers can use optional FORM GSTR-1A once per period to fix GSTR-1 mistakes before filing GSTR-3B [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 59(1) Proviso & 59(4A), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter8/rule59_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. From October 2024, the Invoice Management System (IMS) lets buyers accept, reject, or keep supplier invoices pending for GSTR-2B [Source: GST Portal Final FAQs on IMS, Overview, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/final_faqs_on_ims_22_09_2024.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Under Sections 37(5) and 39(11), GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B cannot be filed more than 3 years after their due date [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 37(5) & Section 39(11), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter9/section39_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. On 8 October 2026, the 57th GST Council recommended waiving late fees on GSTR-3B for taxpayers with turnover up to Rs. 5 crore if filed by the end of the due month, pending notification [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 2320934, Para 15, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2320934, accessed 2026-11-20].
