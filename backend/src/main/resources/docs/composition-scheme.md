# GST Composition Scheme for Small Taxpayers

## Who is eligible for the Composition Scheme
Under Section 10(1) of the CGST Act, 2017, small manufacturers, traders (retailers/wholesalers), and restaurants not serving alcohol can opt for the Composition Scheme [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10(1), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Goods suppliers under Section 10(1) may also provide marginal services up to 10% of their previous-year turnover in the State/UT or Rs. 5 lakh, whichever is higher [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Second Proviso to Section 10(1), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Standalone service providers or mixed suppliers who do not qualify under Section 10(1) can opt in under Section 10(2A) [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10(2A), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. If you have multiple GSTINs under the same PAN, all of them must opt into the scheme together [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Provisos to Section 10(2) & 10(2A), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Turnover limits for the Composition Scheme
Under Section 10(1) and Notification No. 14/2019-Central Tax dated 7 March 2019, the preceding financial year aggregate turnover limit is Rs. 1.5 crore for most States and UTs [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 1567975, Notification No. 14/2019-CT, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1567975, accessed 2026-11-20] [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 2279318, Nine Years of GST, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2279318&reg=3&lang=1, accessed 2026-11-20]. The limit is Rs. 75 lakh in 8 Special Category States: Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, and Uttarakhand [Source: GST Portal Welcome Kit for New Taxpayers, Section 2.3, p. 14, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/welcome_kit_for_new_taxpyers.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Under Section 10(2A), the limit for service providers and mixed suppliers is Rs. 50 lakh across all States and UTs [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10(2A), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Exempt bank interest or discount income is excluded when calculating turnover, and eligibility ends the exact day turnover crosses the limit during the year [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10(3) & Explanation 1, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Composition Scheme tax rates
Under Rule 7 of the CGST Rules, 2017 (substituted vide Notification No. 50/2020-Central Tax dated 24 June 2020), the total effective tax rates (CGST + equal SGST/UTGST) are fixed by business type [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 7, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]:
- **Traders / retailers (Section 10(1))**: 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) of the turnover of *taxable* supplies of goods and services in the State/UT (exempt sales are excluded) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 7 Sl. No. 3, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].
- **Manufacturers (Section 10(1))**: 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) of total turnover in the State/UT [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 7 Sl. No. 1, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].
- **Restaurants not serving alcohol (Section 10(1))**: 5% (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) of total turnover in the State/UT [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 7 Sl. No. 2, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].
- **Service providers / mixed suppliers (Section 10(2A))**: 6% (3% CGST + 3% SGST) of turnover in the State/UT [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 7 Sl. No. 4, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Main conditions and restrictions
Under Section 10(2) and Section 10(4) of the CGST Act, 2017, a Composition taxpayer cannot make any inter-State outward supplies of goods or services, cannot supply non-taxable items like petrol or alcohol, cannot collect GST from customers, and cannot claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10(2)(b)–(c) & Section 10(4), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Casual and non-resident taxable persons are also ineligible [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10(2)(f), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Under Rule 5(1)(f)–(g), you must write "composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies" at the top of every Bill of Supply and display "composition taxable person" on your shop signboard [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 5(1)(f)–(g), https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. If you become ineligible, you must file FORM GST CMP-04 within 7 days [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 6(3), https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/24092021-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Ineligible manufacturers and e-commerce rules
Manufacturers of notified goods cannot opt for the Composition Scheme under Section 10(2)(e), including ice cream (HSN 2105 00 00), pan masala (HSN 2106 90 20), tobacco and tobacco substitutes (Chapter 24), aerated water (HSN 2202 10 10), and fly ash bricks, aggregates, or blocks (Notification No. 14/2019-CT as amended by Notification No. 16/2022-CT dated 13-07-2022) [Source: GST Portal Registration FAQs — Goods and Services, Ineligible goods list, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/registration/Goods_and_Services.htm, accessed 2026-11-20] [Source: Notification No. 16/2022-Central Tax dated 13-07-2022, Table Sl. No. 4, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/central-tax/16-2022-ct-eng.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Effective 1 October 2023 (Finance Act, 2023 via Notification No. 28/2023-CT dated 31-07-2023), Composition dealers are allowed to sell goods within the same State through e-commerce operators, though supplying services through TCS-collecting e-commerce operators and inter-State sales remain prohibited [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 10 Footnotes 12–13, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter3/section10_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20] [Source: GST Portal Form GSTR-8 FAQs, Composition intra-State rule, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/FAQs_GSTR-8.htm, accessed 2026-11-20].
