# GST Invoicing Basics for Small Shops

## When a tax invoice is required
Under Section 31(1) of the CGST Act, 2017, a regular registered shop supplying taxable goods must issue a tax invoice before or at the time of removing the goods (if movement is involved) or delivering/making them available to the buyer [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 31(1)(a)–(b), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter7/section31_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. For taxable services, Section 31(2) and Rule 47 require issuing a tax invoice before or after supplying the service, within 30 days of supply (45 days for banks and insurers) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 47, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule47_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Under Section 31(3)(f) and Rule 47A (effective 1 November 2024 via Notification No. 20/2024-CT), if you buy goods or services from an unregistered supplier under Reverse Charge, you must issue a self-invoice within 30 days of receipt [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 47A, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule47a_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Small-value sales below Rs. 200
Under Section 31(3)(b) of the CGST Act, 2017 and the Third Active Proviso to Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017, you do not have to issue a separate tax invoice for each sale below Rs. 200 if two conditions are met: the buyer is unregistered and the buyer does not ask for an invoice [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 31(3)(b), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter7/section31_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20] [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46 Proviso, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Instead, you must prepare one consolidated tax invoice at the close of each day covering all such small sales [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46 Proviso, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Mandatory supplier and buyer details on a tax invoice (Rule 46)
Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017 requires every tax invoice to show: your shop's name, address, and GSTIN (clause (a)); a consecutive serial number of up to 16 characters using letters, numbers, `-`, or `/`, unique for the financial year (clause (b)); and the date of issue (clause (c)) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46(a)–(c), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. If the buyer is GST-registered, include their name, address, and GSTIN/UIN (clause (d)) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46(d), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. If the buyer is unregistered and the taxable value is Rs. 50,000 or more—or below Rs. 50,000 if the buyer requests it—include their name, address, delivery address, and State name with code (clauses (e)–(f)) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46(e)–(f), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Mandatory item, HSN code, and tax details (Rule 46)
Rule 46 also requires: HSN code for goods or services (clause (g)); description of items (clause (h)); quantity and unit/UQC for goods (clause (i)); total value (clause (j)); taxable value after discounts (clause (k)); tax rate for CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST, or cess (clause (l)); tax amount charged (clause (m)); place of supply and State name for inter-State sales (clause (n)); delivery address if different from place of supply (clause (o)); and whether tax is payable on reverse charge (clause (p)) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46(g)–(p), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Under Notification No. 78/2020-Central Tax dated 15 October 2020, shops with previous-year turnover up to Rs. 5 crore must quote 4-digit HSN on B2B invoices (not mandatory in GSTR-1 Table 12 for B2C), while turnover above Rs. 5 crore requires 6-digit HSN [Source: CBIC Press Release on HSN/SAC Code, Effective 01-04-2021, https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/press-release/Press-release-HSN-code-SAC-issue.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20] [Source: GST Portal Advisory on HSN Table 12 dated 25-04-2025, Table 12 requirements, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/updated_advisory_hsn_table12_25042025.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Signature, QR code, and special declarations (Rule 46)
A tax invoice must carry the signature or digital signature of the supplier or authorised representative (clause (q)), unless issued as an electronic invoice under the Information Technology Act, 2000 [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46(q) & Proviso, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Where e-invoicing under Rule 48(4) applies, the invoice must include a QR code with an embedded IRN (clause (r)) [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46(r), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Taxpayers whose turnover crossed the e-invoicing limit in any year from 2017-18 onwards but who are exempt from e-invoicing must print the Rule 46(s) exemption declaration [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46(s), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].

## When a Bill of Supply or Invoice-cum-Bill of Supply is used
Under Section 31(3)(c) of the CGST Act, 2017 and Rule 49 of the CGST Rules, 2017, issue a Bill of Supply instead of a Tax Invoice when you sell exempted goods or services, or when you pay tax under the Composition Scheme (Section 10) [Source: CGST Act, 2017, Section 31(3)(c), https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter7/section31_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. A Bill of Supply must list supplier details, serial number (up to 16 characters), date, registered buyer details (if any), HSN code, description, net value after discount, and signature (Rule 49(a)–(h)), and shares the same Rs. 200 daily consolidated rule [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 49(a)–(h) & First Proviso, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule49_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20]. Under Rule 46A, if you sell both taxable and exempted items together to an unregistered customer, you may issue one combined "invoice-cum-bill of supply" containing the particulars of Rules 46 and 49 [Source: CGST Rules, 2017, Rule 46A, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46a_v1.00.html, accessed 2026-11-20].
