# GST Rates and Common Shop Items

## Current GST rate slabs in India
The old four-slab GST structure (5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%) was replaced effective 22 September 2025 following the 56th GST Council Meeting held on 3 September 2025 [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 2163555, Recommendations of 56th GST Council, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2163555, accessed 2026-11-20]. General goods now follow a two-rate structure: a 5% merit rate (Schedule I) and an 18% standard rate (Schedule II), plus a 40% special rate (Schedule III) for luxury and de-merit goods like aerated drinks and large cars [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedules I–III, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Special rates also exist for precious metals and stones: 3% (Schedule IV), 0.25% (Schedule V), and 1.50% (Schedule VI) [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedules IV–VI, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. For intra-State sales, the total rate splits equally into CGST and SGST [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Opening para, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Exempt and nil-rated goods
Essential items like fresh produce, unpacked loose grains, and basic school stationery are exempt from GST (0%) under Notification No. 10/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17 September 2025, which replaced Notification No. 2/2017-Central Tax (Rate) effective 22 September 2025 [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Cross-reference to Notif. 10/2025-CT(R), https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. A 28% rate (Schedule VII) was retained only temporarily on 22 September 2025 for pan masala and tobacco products until compensation cess loan obligations are cleared [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 2163560, FAQs on 56th GST Council decisions, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163560, accessed 2026-11-20]. The 57th GST Council Meeting on 8 October 2026 made no change to the 5%, 18%, and 40% slab structure [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 2320934, 57th GST Council Meeting recommendations, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2320934, accessed 2026-11-20].

## How to find the GST rate for any shop item
Go to https://www.gst.gov.in, open Services > User Services > Search HSN Code, and search by entering at least 3 digits of the HSN code or 3 characters of the product name (such as "biscuit" or "soap") [Source: GST Portal User Manual — Search HSN Code, Search steps, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/taxpayersdashboard/Search_HSN_SAC_Tax_Rates_manual.htm, accessed 2026-11-20]. You can also click "Download HSN Directory in Excel Format" on that page to save the full HSN list offline [Source: GST Portal User Manual — Search HSN Code, Offline download, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/taxpayersdashboard/Search_HSN_SAC_Tax_Rates_manual.htm, accessed 2026-11-20]. Because portal search results are only for guidance and are not legally binding, always confirm the final tax rate against the official CBIC rate notifications published on https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in or https://egazette.gov.in [Source: GST Portal User Manual — Search HSN Code, Legal note, https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/taxpayersdashboard/Search_HSN_SAC_Tax_Rates_manual.htm, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Verified rates for packaged snacks
Under Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17 September 2025 (effective 22 September 2025), namkeens, bhujia, mixture, and chabena (HSN 2106 90) attract 5% GST (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST), reduced from 12% [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule I, S. No. 143, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Extruded or expanded savoury/salted snacks (HSN 1905 / 1905 90 30) attract 5% GST, reduced from 12% [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule I, S. No. 123, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Biscuits, cakes, and pastries (HSN 1905) also attract 5% GST, reduced from 18% [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule I, S. No. 123, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Verified rates for stationery items
Effective 22 September 2025, exercise books, graph books, laboratory notebooks, and notebooks (HSN 4820), pencils and crayons (HSN 9608/9609), erasers (HSN 4016), and pencil sharpeners (HSN 8214) were reduced to Nil (0%) GST [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 2163555, Annexures I & II, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2163555, accessed 2026-11-20]. Geometry and mathematical boxes (HSN 7310/7326) attract 5% GST under Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17 September 2025 [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule I, S. No. 410, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Ballpoint pens, fountain pens, markers, and pen parts (HSN 9608) attract 18% GST [Source: Notification No. 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule II, S. No. 626, https://courier.cbic.gov.in/ECCS/advisory/2025/NOTIFICATION%20NO.%209_2025-INTEGRATED%20TAX%20(RATE)%20-1759486719.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Registers, account books, diaries, and folders (HSN 4820) also remain at 18% GST [Source: Notification No. 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule II, S. No. 186, https://courier.cbic.gov.in/ECCS/advisory/2025/NOTIFICATION%20NO.%209_2025-INTEGRATED%20TAX%20(RATE)%20-1759486719.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Pre-packaged versus loose grocery staples
Staples like rice (HSN 1006), wheat (HSN 1001), atta/maida (HSN 1101), and pulses (HSN 0713) attract 5% GST only when sold "pre-packaged and labelled" in retail packs of 25 kg or less (or 25 litres or less) under Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17 September 2025 [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule I, S. Nos. 20, 43, 48, 51, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. This 25 kg/25 litre retail limit was clarified in the 55th GST Council and Explanation (a)(ii) of Notification No. 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate) [Source: Notification No. 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Explanation (a)(ii), https://courier.cbic.gov.in/ECCS/advisory/2025/NOTIFICATION%20NO.%209_2025-INTEGRATED%20TAX%20(RATE)%20-1759486719.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Loose staples weighed in front of the customer and single bulk bags above 25 kg are exempt (0%), though a master bag containing smaller retail packs of 25 kg or less attracts 5% GST [Source: PIB Press Release PRID 1842250, FAQs on pre-packaged and labelled goods, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=1842250, accessed 2026-11-20].

## Verified rates for other daily grocery items
Under Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17 September 2025 (effective 22 September 2025), edible oils (HSN 1507–1516, S. Nos. 84–93), sugar (HSN 1701, S. No. 105), and tea (HSN 0902, S. No. 34) attract 5% GST [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule I, S. Nos. 34, 84–93, 105, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Toilet soap bars and cakes (HSN 3401, S. No. 251) were reduced from 18% to 5% GST [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule I, S. No. 251, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20]. Liquid soaps, handwash (HSN 3401, S. No. 66), and washing detergents (HSN 3402, S. No. 67) remain at 18% GST [Source: Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17-09-2025, Schedule II, S. Nos. 66–67, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf, accessed 2026-11-20].
